# 08 — Demo Script and Q&A

## 90-second product demo script

### Opening
Cancer research labs receive increasingly complex mutation datasets. The hard part is not only sequencing; it is turning mutation data into useful research decisions.

BioQuantum Oncology helps oncology R&D teams move from mutation data to a ranked drug-repurposing shortlist faster.

### Step 1 — Upload dataset
Here, the researcher uploads a de-identified mutation dataset or selects a demo dataset. In a real pilot, this would come from a cancer genomics lab or research sequencing workflow.

### Step 2 — Analysis workflow
The platform standardises mutation data, maps genes to cancer pathways, ranks mutation relevance, matches drug-target relationships, and applies a hybrid AI-quantum ranking layer.

### Step 3 — Mutation results
The researcher sees the most relevant mutation signals. For example, EGFR and KRAS mutations are linked to important cancer signalling pathways, while TP53 relates to tumour suppressor function.

### Step 4 — Drug-repurposing shortlist
The platform ranks existing drug classes or candidates that may be worth investigating. These are not treatment recommendations; they are research hypotheses.

### Step 5 — Explainability
For every candidate, the platform explains why it was ranked: matched gene, pathway, evidence level, confidence score, and limitation.

### Step 6 — Report
Finally, the lab gets a research-use report for internal discussion, validation planning, and possible collaboration with pharma or genomics partners.

## 30-second version
BioQuantum Oncology is a research-use oncology analytics platform. A lab uploads de-identified mutation data, and our hybrid AI-quantum workflow ranks mutation-risk signals and repurposable drug hypotheses with explainable evidence. We start with paid research pilots for cancer labs and genomics companies, then convert validated pilots into SaaS and enterprise licensing.

## Q&A answers

### Is this a diagnostic product?
No. Phase 1 is research-use only. It does not diagnose cancer or recommend treatment. Clinical use would require future regulatory validation.

### Why quantum?
The quantum layer is a benchmarked compatibility-ranking module for complex mutation-drug-target relationships. We do not claim guaranteed quantum advantage. We will compare hybrid AI-quantum ranking against classical AI baselines.

### Who pays first?
Cancer research labs, genomics service providers, and biotech/pharma R&D teams. They already work with mutation data and need faster hypothesis generation.

### What exactly are you selling?
A research analytics platform that provides mutation relevance ranking, drug-repurposing shortlist, explainable evidence, API/dashboard access, and exportable research reports.

### Why not hospitals first?
Hospitals require clinical validation, regulatory approvals, and integration with clinical workflows. Research labs are a faster first market because the product can be used as non-diagnostic decision support.

### What makes this different from normal AI dashboards?
The differentiation is the protected hybrid AI-quantum workflow applied to mutation analysis, cancer risk signal ranking, and drug-repurposing prioritisation, with explainability and validation planning.

### What is your 6-month milestone?
Three paid research pilots, one validated case study, one benchmark against classical AI baselines, and one annual SaaS conversion.

### What is the main risk?
Overclaiming clinical utility too early. We reduce this by starting as research-use only and validating retrospectively before any clinical pathway.
